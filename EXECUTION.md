## Customer interview prompts

Ask about recent behaviour before showing the offer. Separate existing crocheters from finished-product buyers.

1. What was the last pattern or home décor item you paid for? What did it cost?
2. What made you choose it, and what was difficult after buying?
3. Have you made a project involving a frame or fitting? How did you find the parts?
4. Which of these three designs would you actually make or buy, and why?
5. For this specific £9 pattern package, what would stop you buying?
6. What proof or information would you need before deciding?

Record objections verbatim where practical. Do not treat hypothetical willingness to pay as an order. Obtain permission before retaining personal details or quoting feedback publicly.
