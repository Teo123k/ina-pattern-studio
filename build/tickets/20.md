# Ticket 20: Deterministic geometry preview

Dependencies: 19.

## Implement

Build a versioned digital-prediction artifact from current confirmed frame/bulb geometry and explicitly declared stitch-surface values. Produce a `.glb` showing the shade envelope and bulb envelope, plus a JSON report with dimensions, calculated aperture/open-area estimate and clearance map.

## Acceptance

Only current confirmed geometry may generate a preview. The stored result must retain its geometry version, checksums and calibration status. All outputs must say they are digital predictions, never safety certification or a physically proven yarn simulation.

## Out of scope

STEP/STL CAD export, Blender cloth/yarn simulation, Cycles rendering, thermal simulation and automatic stitch inference from images remain separate future work. A swatch and a completed physical prototype remain the evidence gates.
